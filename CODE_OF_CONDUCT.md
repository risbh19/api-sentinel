# Community code of conduct

Help participants investigate problems and learn. Give specific technical feedback, respect contributors' time, and credit their work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted contact and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately. If a private contact is unavailable or the complaint involves that maintainer, use GitHub's user or content reporting controls. Keep personal details out of public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation or block accounts. They should consider context and impact, protect the reporter's privacy and explain moderation decisions privately where practical.
